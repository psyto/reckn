The seller reads two values before working: the deal's verifier and its verifierCodeHash. The buyer names the adjudicating program at funding, so a buyer who names one that always returns Failed makes the seller work for nothing — and on-chain that is indistinguishable from an honest Failed. Nothing checks it for them. That is a cost of letting the funder choose the program, and it is stated here rather than in a footnote.
  • The escrow has no timeout: if no proof arrives, a funded deal stays funded (task 003).
  • “No bridge, no light client” describes the path that decides the payout — not anchoring.
