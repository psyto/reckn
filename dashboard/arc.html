1 · An honest delivery

Fund 250.00 USDC against the EVM guest's verifier, then release it with the real Groth16 proof that the work reproduces.

2 · Try to steal it

Submit a different execution's proof against the funded deal. It verifies — it is a real proof — and the binding is what stops it.

3 · The work did not reproduce

A proof that the balance decreased. Before this repository closed a soundness bug, this exact execution paid the seller.

4 · The work happened on Solana

USDC escrowed on Arc, released by a proof about work performed on Solana. No bridge, no light client, no resolver.

5 · Nobody ever proves anything

A funded deal whose prover never shows up. Refund early and it refuses; after thirty days anyone may return the money to the buyer, and the caller gets nothing for it.

What is on this chain

—

USDC on Arc is the native gas token; Circle exposes an ERC-20 face over the same balance at 0x3600…0000, six decimals. The escrow holds that.

What no button here can do: move a funded deal without a proof that carries its binding, or before the deadline. There is no address with that power — not the buyer's, not the seller's, not the deployer's, because there is no deployer role and no constructor.
  • The buyer names the adjudicating program at funding. A buyer who names one that always returns Failed makes the seller work for nothing, and on-chain that is indistinguishable from an honest Failed. Sellers read the deal first.
  • “No bridge, no light client” describes the path that decides the payout — not anchoring.
