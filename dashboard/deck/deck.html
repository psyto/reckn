Reckn

Keep assets native.
Settle on proof.

An escrow that releases only when the work it was funded against can be re-executed and reproduced — and refunds the buyer when it cannot.

Live on Arc testnet. psyto.github.io/reckn
The claim

Nobody can move this money.
Including us.

No owner, no admin, no resolver, no upgrade path. That is a build condition, not a promise — if one ever appears, the build fails.

Easy to say. So don't take our word for it.
