02
The inversion

Don't move the asset to reach the work.
Move a proof of the work to reach the asset.

The buyer escrows the stablecoin on the chain it already lives on. The seller works wherever the work belongs. The disputed execution is replayed — release if it reproduces, refund if it does not.

Don't make a bridge decide where money goes. Make proof decide.

07
What this does not claim

The half that is easy to leave out.

Not“Bridges are unnecessary.” It removes the bridge as the decider. Moving funds to where you pay is a different problem.
Not“Fragmented liquidity is solved.” You must already hold the settlement asset on the chain you pay from.
Not“We prove Solana mainnet state.” The guest recomputes a bank hash over the account set the deal named. Consistency, not provenance.
Stated on the live page, in the film, and in a test — not in a footnote.
