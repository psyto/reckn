Reckn

Keep assets native.
Settle on proof.

An escrow that releases only when the work it was funded against can be re-executed and reproduced — and refunds the buyer when it cannot.

Reckn is building the standard for proof-driven settlement across execution environments. Agents may choose where work happens. Assets remain native. Reproducible execution decides payout.

Live on Arc testnet. psyto.github.io/reckn

Why Arc

Arc is where the dollar stays.

The work happened on Solana. The USDC never left Arc. A conditional payment settles on a stablecoin-native rail without the asset being bridged anywhere.

Reckn removes a protocol-level judge. It does not erase issuer policy.

Why Tempo

Not just another EVM deployment.

Tempo has no native gas token. The escrow holds a stablecoin — and the fee that releases it is paid in that same stablecoin. Read off the receipt, not asserted.

What it does not prove.

Not“Bridges are unnecessary.”It removes the bridge as the decider.
Not“Liquidity is solved.”You still hold funds where you pay.
Not“We prove Solana mainnet.”Consistency, not provenance.
